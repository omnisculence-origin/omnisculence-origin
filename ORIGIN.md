# Omnisculence

## Dictionary Entry

**Pronunciation:** /ˌɒm.nɪˈskjuː.ləns/  
**Part of speech:** noun  
**Grammatical form:** uncountable  
**Adjective form:** omnisculent  
**Derived form:** omnisculently

**Definition:**  
A complex, unified emotional state in which multiple, often contradictory emotions are experienced simultaneously and cannot be reduced to a single identifiable feeling or expressed through a single linguistic category.

**Etymology:**  
Coined term formed from omni meaning “all” and the stylized suffix sculence, constructed to denote emotional convergence.

**Origin:**  
Coined by Enad, Pacheco  
Date: 04 February 2026  
Location: Philippines

**Usage notes:**  
Used to describe emotional experiences characterized by emotional plurality, internal contradiction, and expressive insufficiency.

**Examples:**  
Winning the lottery felt like omnisculence.  
Her reaction to the reunion was pure omnisculence.  
Graduation day was filled with omnisculence.
